# Privacy policy

**Last updated: October 21, 2026.**

This policy describes the third-party services incorporated into the **serialcraft.xyz** documentation website.

## Site operator and hosting

SerialCraft is maintained as an independent project by **Leonardo Aliaga**. Visit the [contact page](/en/contact) for public channels.

This website primarily serves static documentation through **GitHub Pages**. GitHub may process connection-related technical information according to its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The website has no proprietary user-account registration or checkout system.

## Google Analytics

**Google Analytics** is blocked until you choose **Accept analytics**. Rejecting analytics does not restrict access to documentation. Use **Cookie settings** on any page to change your choice. Depending on applicable settings and consent, third-party tools can process browsing events, device attributes, identifiers and approximate location. Read [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Advertising and AdSense

Advertising is currently inactive. An AdSense verification meta tag is included; it does not load ads or advertising scripts. Before activating advertising, the site operator must configure and verify a Google-certified CMP where required. The analytics notice does not collect advertising consent.

When advertising is enabled, Google and other advertising providers may use cookies based on prior visits to this or other websites, or other identifiers to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalize advertising. See [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Cookies, choice and consent

Your browser allows you to restrict or delete cookies. We store your acceptance or rejection of analytics locally in this browser (`serialcraft.analytics-consent.v1`) for **180 days**. This preference is not sent to a server operated by SerialCraft. Clearing browser data or using another browser resets this choice. If local storage is unavailable, the notice may appear again.

Rejecting analytics blocks its loading. Withdrawing earlier permission disables measurement, removes Analytics cookies accessible to this site, and reloads the page to unload the tag. Withdrawal does not delete data previously sent to Google. The documentation may also store your visual theme preference locally.


## External links

Links to GitHub, Arduino, Fabric and other services open external sites whose policies are outside SerialCraft's control.

## Changes and questions

This policy may change when services or features change. Use the [contact page](/en/contact) to identify the available channels; do not place private personal information in public GitHub issues.
