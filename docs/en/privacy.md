# Privacy policy

**Last updated: October 8, 2026.**

This policy describes the third-party services incorporated into the **serialcraft.xyz** documentation website.

## Site operator and hosting

SerialCraft is maintained as an independent project by **Leonardo Aliaga**. Visit the [contact page](/en/contact) for public channels.

This website primarily serves static documentation through **GitHub Pages**. GitHub may process connection-related technical information according to its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The website has no proprietary user-account registration or checkout system.

## Google Analytics

The site includes **Google Analytics** for measuring page activity. Depending on applicable settings and consent, third-party tools can process browsing events, device attributes, identifiers and approximate location. Read [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Advertising and AdSense

The site includes a **Google AdSense script** while pursuing possible ad monetization. Its presence does **not** indicate that AdSense has approved the site or that ads are currently displayed.

Google and advertising providers may use cookies or other identifiers to serve, measure and, where permitted, personalize advertising. See [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/).

## Cookies, choice and consent

Your browser allows you to restrict or delete cookies. Where applicable privacy law requires prior consent, an appropriate consent mechanism must be implemented before nonessential tracking or advertising technologies are activated. This policy alone is not a replacement for consent.

## External links

Links to GitHub, Arduino, Fabric and other services open external sites whose policies are outside SerialCraft's control.

## Changes and questions

This policy may change when services or features change. Use the [contact page](/en/contact) to identify the available channels; do not place private personal information in public GitHub issues.
